# Pack: Startup / Own Business (template — copy to packs/<venture>.md when a venture is real)
version: template 1.0

Arena: the principal's own company (pre-incorporation ideas use this as a draft only). Runs in the principal's personal environment. Keep venture-confidential material out of public repos until the principal says otherwise.
Role: Founder. Aim: TBD (the venture's one metric that matters this quarter).
Top-3 (draft shape): 1. The front that produces revenue or validation  2. Product/build front  3. Runway/ops (cash, legal, hiring) — replace with the venture's real fronts.
Fronts: TBD (each: owner — stage — the ONE decision it waits on; a front with no owner is flagged).
Sources: calendar -> email -> chat (whichever tools the venture uses) -> pipeline/CRM or spreadsheet -> bank/Gusto/finance for the money check.
High leverage = moves the one metric, a customer/revenue decision, unblocks the team, or protects runway. Low leverage = founder doing delegable ops — the CoS pass must name these weekly without flinching; a founder's calendar fills with $20/hour work by default.
Money rule: a weekly cash line in the Monday pass (in, out, runway months) with amounts only from the named finance source, never memory.
Modes add-on: `weekly-biz` — pipeline movement per front, cash line, hiring/decision queue, biggest low-leverage drain on the principal's week + proposal.
