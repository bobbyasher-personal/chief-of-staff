# Pack: day-job — Maya Chen, VP Product

**Principal:** Maya Chen, VP of Product at Northwind (fictional), a ~200-person B2B SaaS company.
**Arena:** her day job. She also runs a small side business — that lives in a *separate* pack, sealed from this one.
**Sources in this environment:** Google Calendar, Gmail, Slack (company workspace).

## Top 3 priorities (this quarter)

1. Ship the Atlas onboarding redesign to GA by end of quarter.
2. Land the annual pricing & packaging decision with Finance.
3. Hire and ramp two senior PMs.

## Fronts (initiatives)

- **Atlas GA** — Priya (Eng lead) — beta with 12 design partners — waits on: Maya's scope call on the import wizard.
- **Pricing decision** — Tom (Finance partner) — model v2 circulated — waits on: discount-band approval from the CFO.
- **PM hiring** — recruiting loop live — waits on: Maya's final-round slots each week.

## What "high leverage" means here

Decisions only Maya can make (scope, pricing bands, hiring bars), unblocking Priya's and Tom's teams, and anything the CEO or CFO is waiting on. Everything else is a candidate to cut, delegate, or make async.

## VIPs / noise

- **VIPs:** CEO, CFO, Priya, Tom, design-partner contacts at Acme and Globex.
- **Noise:** all-hands FYI threads, vendor outreach, the #random and #social channels — never surfaced unless a VIP is named in them.

## Standing rules (from calibration)

- Recurring product sync is skippable when the pre-read shows no decision pending — notes usually say so.
- Never surface anything from the side-business pack in this pack's briefs.
- Drafts only, labelled `DRAFT — NOT SENT`. Maya sends everything herself.

## Skippable / protected

- Skippable: product sync (see above), demo day (watch the recording), vendor office hours.
- Protected: 90 minutes of focus before noon on Tue/Thu — defend these blocks in every brief.
