# Pack: PanelDesk (example)
version: example 1.0, filled as of "second quarter of operations"

Arena: Marcus's own company — a (fictional) job-costing tool for small electrical contractors. Runs in his personal environment. Venture-confidential: nothing from this pack goes in a public repo.
> *Why this fill works: even a solo founder's pack states its boundary and what the venture is in one line — six months later, that line is the fastest way to re-orient.*

Role: Founder (solo + 2 contractors: one engineer, one part-time designer).
Aim: the one metric this quarter — **40 paying contractor firms** (currently 17). Everything else is commentary until that number moves.
> *Shape: ONE metric, with the current value next to the target. A startup pack with three "one metrics" has none.*

Top-3:
1. Revenue/validation — convert the 9 firms in active trials before their trials lapse
2. Product/build — ship automated invoice-to-job matching (the feature 6 of 9 trial accounts asked for)
3. Runway/ops — keep runway ≥ 12 months; close the bookkeeping handoff so Marcus stops doing it
> *Shape: the template's draft shape made real — the revenue front names its actual pipeline, the build front names the feature customers asked for (not the founder's favourite), and runway carries a floor number.*

Fronts:
- Trial conversions — owner: Marcus — stage: 9 in trial, 3 in week 3+ — waits on: pricing call for accounts above 25 jobs/month (Marcus decides this week)
- Invoice matching v1 — owner: contract engineer — stage: building — waits on: sample invoice sets from 2 trial accounts (they owe)
- Bookkeeping handoff — owner: Marcus — stage: accountant shortlisted — waits on: Marcus signing the engagement letter (sat 6 days)
> *Shape: same owner — stage — one-decision discipline as the employer pack. Note the third front: the waiting decision is the founder himself — a good pack flags that without flinching.*

Sources (in order): Calendar → email → chat (the venture's workspace) → pipeline spreadsheet → bank + accounting tool for the money check.
> *Shape: the finance source is named, because the money rule below forbids amounts from anywhere else.*

High leverage = moves the 40-account metric, a customer/revenue decision, unblocks the engineer, or protects runway. Low leverage = Marcus reconciling invoices by hand, redesigning the landing page (designer's job, briefed once), any admin a $30/hr bookkeeper can do.
> *Shape: the founder-specific low-leverage list is the honest one — named tasks, not categories. The Monday pass names the week's biggest drain from this list.*

Money rule (applied): the Monday pass carries the cash line — in, out, runway months — from the bank/accounting source only. This week's example line: *In $6,120 · Out $9,480 · Runway 13.5 months.*
> *Shape: a worked money line, so "weekly cash line" is a format, not a slogan. Amounts always traced to the named source, never memory.*

Modes add-on applied: `weekly-biz` runs Mondays — pipeline movement per front (trials: +2 started, 1 lapsed), the cash line above, the decision queue (pricing call, engagement letter), and the biggest low-leverage drain last week (4.5 hrs manual reconciliation) + the proposal (bookkeeper takes it Friday).
> *Shape: the add-on shown as output, not description — a founder reading this knows exactly what Monday morning will look like.*
