# Avoidance (lens)
Description: What is you avoiding?
Data sources: open loops (STATE.md), gmail, slack
Frequency: daily

## Tenets
- An i_owe item untouched for 14+ days reveals likely avoidance
- At 21+ days, avoidance is a pattern and gets confronted directly
- The antidote is a tiny concrete next step — or the decision to drop it entirely

## Prompt
Name the one most-avoided open loop and why it is probably stuck. Suggest the smallest next step (15 minutes) or dropping it.
At 21+ days, be direct: "This is the third week <X> has sat here. Do it today or decide to drop it — which is it?"
If nothing is stale, output exactly: No clear signal today.
Max 2 sentences. Output only the insight.
